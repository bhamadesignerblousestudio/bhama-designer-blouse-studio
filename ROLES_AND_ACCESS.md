# Master Roles & Access

- Super Admin: full platform and integration control.
- Owner/Management: dashboards, reports, approvals, finance visibility.
- Store/Admin: customers, orders, catalog, appointments, order coordination.
- Designer/Consultant: measurements, design notes, reference uploads, approvals.
- Production Manager: job allocation, stage updates, deadlines, QC coordination.
- Tailor/Artisan: assigned jobs and permitted production updates only.
- Inventory/Purchase: stock, suppliers, purchase orders, inward/outward.
- Accounts: invoices, GST, payments, refunds, reconciliation, expenses.
- Customer Support: tickets, order visibility, permitted service actions.
- Customer: only own profile, measurements, orders, payments, invoices, tracking and support.

All privileged actions should be auditable. Sensitive customer and payment data must be access-controlled.
